# Code of Conduct

This project should remain a respectful, practical space for collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still reviewing technical risk carefully.
- Keep discussion focused on evidence, safety, maintainability, and user value.
- Respect privacy and never request or share real patient data in this project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Introducing real patient data, credentials, or unsafe clinical claims.
- Repeatedly ignoring documented safety and data-handling boundaries.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through the project's
normal private contact channel. Maintainers may remove harmful content, close
issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when needed to protect the
project and its contributors.
